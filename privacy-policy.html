Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Notice describes:

  • the information that Freecierge Co. (“we”, “us”) collects from you in any form, including when you interact with our website (“Website”);
  • how we collect, use and disclose the information that we receive, including sharing that information with third parties; and
  • how we store and secure information.

How to Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns or complaints about our Privacy Notice or our data collection or processing practices, or if you want to report any suspected security violations to us, please contact us at the following email address:

Services We Provide

We offer the following services (“Services”) to our customers:

  • Brand Sprints — brand strategy, identity and messaging
  • Product Sprints — AI-assisted prototyping, validation and web and iOS development
  • Venture partnerships with AI-powered startups
  • AI upskilling, workshops and concierge support

Information We Collect

Information You Provide to Us. We will collect data from you that you voluntarily provide.

  • Communications Information. We collect information you provide to us on our contact and apply forms, such as your name, email address, company, a description of your business or idea, links to your website or content, and the reason for contacting us.
  • Contact Us / Support. If you give us feedback, email us or contact us for support, we collect your name and email address, as well as any other content included in the message, in order to send you a reply.
  • Files You Share. If you upload a file, such as a pitch deck, we receive and store that file so we can review your application.
  • Subscriptions. If you subscribe to our updates, we collect your email address.
  • Confirmation Emails. When you submit a form or subscribe, we use your email address (and your name, if you gave one) to send you a one-time confirmation message that we received your request.

Information Collected via Technology.

  • Automatically Collected. When you interact with our Website, our hosting provider may automatically collect certain technical information about your device and browsing actions, such as your Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, browser type, operating system, pages viewed and referring page. We use this to keep the Website secure and working properly.
  • Browser Storage. The Website uses your browser’s session storage only to remember that you moved between pages so that page transitions play correctly. It contains no personal information and is cleared when you close the tab.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries, applications and booking requests.
  • To send you an automatic confirmation that your message, application or subscription was received.
  • To decide whether and how we can work together, and to deliver the Services you request.
  • To send updates you have asked for. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the Website.
  • To meet legal obligations and protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to make automated decisions that significantly affect you.

Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will describe it here and, where required, ask for your consent first. You can block or delete cookies and site storage in your browser settings; the Website will continue to work.

Sharing Your Information

We share information only where needed to run the studio, and only with providers who are bound to protect it:

  • Service providers that host the Website, deliver email or process forms.
  • Web3Forms (web3forms.com) receives what you type into our contact, apply and subscribe forms and delivers it to our email inbox.
  • EmailJS (emailjs.com) sends the automatic confirmation email to the address you give us. That confirmation only thanks you; it does not repeat what you submitted.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers and accountants, where necessary.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

Web3Forms and EmailJS process the information you submit only to deliver and send these emails for us. They are separate companies and may store it on servers outside your country; their own privacy policies describe how they handle data. Please do not include sensitive personal information (such as financial or health details) in a form.

The Website loads fonts and code libraries from third-party content networks (for example Google Fonts and public CDNs), so your browser connects to those providers and they may see your IP address. The “Get an AI summary of this page” links open ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity in a new tab; once you leave our Website, those services’ own privacy policies apply.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep enquiry and application details for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a business relationship, and typically no longer than 24 months after our last contact unless you become a client or the law requires us to keep records longer. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including access controls and encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent you have given. To exercise any of these rights, email us at freecierge0705@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and, where required, within the period set by law. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

The Website is intended for businesses and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.
