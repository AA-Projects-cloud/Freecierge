How we work

Your product is a hypothesis — our process proves it.

Our Product Sprint is a structured, high-speed engagement that takes a raw idea to a validated, working product. With AI-driven research and rapid prototyping we cut the busywork, not the quality. Here’s how it works:

Book a sprint
01Week 1

Discover

We map the problem, the users and the market. You complete a short intake, then we run a Product Workshop to define the one thing your first version must prove.

02Week 2

Prototype

We turn the idea into a clickable, testable prototype using AI-assisted design — enough fidelity to put in front of real users within days.

03Week 3-4

Validate

We run user tests and fast experiments, measure what matters, and decide with you what to cut, keep and double down on.

04Week 5-6

Build

We ship the validated version — web and iOS — with clean architecture, analytics and a roadmap so your team can keep moving.
